Our purpose

About UniWork

UniWork is a student-focused jobs platform for Uganda, designed to help university students discover internships and flexible work while helping employers connect with emerging talent.

Listings marked as samples are examples only and cannot receive applications. Employer-submitted listings become available only after the site owner approves them.

Application information

What happens when you apply

Your student account identity, selected role, optional message, status, and submission time are stored by the site. The employer who owns that role can see your name and email address, message, and application status so they can respond to you. Other employers cannot view your application. The site owner can operate and moderate the platform.

Applications are unavailable on sample listings. Account creation currently does not send email verification or password-reset messages. For account help or a data request, use the official privacy contact published by the site operator. The offline app cache contains public page files only; it excludes account and application API responses.

Start with care

For students

Explore roles that suit your skills, interests, location, and study schedule. Before sharing personal information or accepting an offer, contact the employer through a channel you can independently verify.

  • Confirm the employer, role, pay, hours, work location, and any costs before you agree.
  • Do not pay a recruiter to apply or to secure an interview without independently checking the request.
  • Share only the information needed for an application, and keep a copy of the role details and your communications.
  • Ask questions about supervision, learning outcomes, and how the work fits with your studies.

Create a student account to apply to approved listings and track your applications. Sample roles are for preview only and do not accept applications.

Work with students

For employers

Write clear, inclusive opportunities that help students make an informed decision. Include responsibilities, required skills, location, schedule, compensation, duration, and the application process.

  • Provide accurate company and role information, and update or close opportunities when they are no longer available.
  • Describe pay, hours, eligibility, and any costs or equipment requirements plainly.
  • Collect only the applicant information you need, secure it, and explain how long you will keep it.
  • Use a fair selection process and communicate next steps promptly.

Create an employer account to submit a listing for owner review. Approved listings appear on the home page; use the printable brief to prepare role details.

Information used by this release

Student and employer accounts store a name, email address, role, and a password hash. The server uses a necessary, HttpOnly session cookie to keep an account signed in for up to seven days. When a student applies, the job, student account, application message, status, and submission time are stored. Employer-submitted listings and their review status are stored so the owner can review and publish them. The server stores this information in its SQLite database; the operator is responsible for securing and backing up that database.

Printable form fields are not submitted to the server. The site loads fonts and a photograph from third-party services; those requests may disclose standard connection information to those providers. This release does not include analytics or advertising tools. Confirm these details against the deployed configuration.

Platform role

UniWork connects student and employer accounts and displays employer-submitted opportunities after owner review. Unless a written agreement says otherwise, the platform does not employ candidates, make hiring decisions, guarantee that a listing is accurate, or promise an interview or job. Sample listings are illustrative and cannot receive applications.

Accounts and applications

Account holders are responsible for keeping their sign-in details private and providing accurate information. Student applications store the applicant account, selected role, optional message, status, and submission time. Employers are responsible for accurate listings and their own recruitment and applicant-data practices. Students should verify opportunities before sharing information or accepting work.

Cookie and storage notice Draft · review required

Update this notice whenever analytics, advertising, sign-in, or other third-party services are added.

After sign-in, UniWork sets a necessary session cookie named uniwork_session. It is HttpOnly, uses SameSite=Strict, and expires after seven days. The Secure attribute is enabled in production when the site is served over HTTPS. Clearing browser site data signs the user out. The local administrator/editor no longer saves page content in browser storage.
